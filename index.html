HELP & PRIVACY

Good to know

Are the tools free?

Yes. The goal is to keep the core utilities free to use without an account.

Do you upload my files?

Available tools process files in your browser. Optional models or libraries may download to your device, but that is separate from uploading your file. Each tool explains its own behavior.

Can I use output for official applications?

Check the preview and compare dimensions, file size, format, and other rules with the latest official notification before you submit.

About Variuno Tools

Variuno Tools is a free collection of simple tools for everyday file jobs: resizing and compressing images, working with PDFs and documents, cleaning text, and preparing photos and signatures for exam and application forms.

Why we built it

Many people, especially students filling in government exam and college forms, struggle with exact photo sizes, PDF size limits and confusing tools that ask them to sign up or upload private documents. We wanted one clear place where these jobs just work.

What we promise

  • Free: no account, subscription or purchase to use the tools.
  • Private: files are processed in your browser and are not uploaded.
  • Honest: each tool says what it can and cannot do. Some conversions keep the text but not the exact layout.

Exam and form requirements

Form rules change often. We link to the official notice where we can, but we are not affiliated with any exam conducting body, university or government office. Always check the latest official notification and the live application portal before you submit.

Found a mistake?

If a requirement looks out of date or a tool does not work for your file, please tell us on the Contact page.

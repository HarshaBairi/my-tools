Live photo capture

Some portals open your webcam or phone camera during the application. Take that picture directly in the official form; an edited file or a photo of a screen/printed photo cannot replace a live capture.

Examples include recent SSC CGL, RRB ALP, JEE Main, NEET UG and some bank applications. Keep good front lighting, plain background, camera at eye level, and follow that portal's frame.

OFFICIAL CYCLE EXAMPLES · CHECKED 26 OCT 2026

Examples from named exam cycles

These are not live application-status updates; some 2026 application windows have closed. The dimensions and file limits below belong to the named notice. Some notices give KB but no pixels, so those presets use a clearly labeled suggested canvas. Check the active portal and source notice before preparing or submitting files.

GATE 2027

Photo: 5–600 KB; JPG; 200×260 to 530×690 px; plain white background and face about 60–70%. Signature: 3–300 KB; 250×80 to 580×180 px, dark ink, not block capitals.

JEE Main 2026

Live photo plus uploaded photo (10–200 KB) and signature (10–100 KB). The bulletin does not specify pixel dimensions; it also lists certificate/ID uploads for applicable candidates.

JEE Main 2026 bulletin ↗

NEET UG 2026

Live photo plus uploaded photo (10–200 KB), signature (10–100 KB), and left/right hand finger and thumb impressions (10–200 KB each). Applicable certificates and proof documents are PDFs with their own limits.

NEET UG 2026 bulletin and notices ↗

SSC CGL 2026

Photo captured live in the application. Scanned signature: JPEG/JPG, 10–20 KB. The 2026 notice gives two different image dimensions: about 6×2 cm in the main requirements and 4×2 cm in the application steps. Confirm the active upload prompt; the preset uses an approximate 6×2 cm canvas.

SSC CGL 2026 official notice ↗

RRB ALP · CEN 01/2026

Live photo in the application. Signature: JPG/JPEG, 30–49 KB, at least 140×60 px; black ink, white paper and running handwriting, centred in a 35×20 mm box.

RRB CEN 01/2026 official notice and FAQ ↗

IBPS-hosted bank recruitment example · 2026

One 2026 Central Bank of India recruitment guide hosted through the IBPS application system specifies photo 200×230 px (20–50 KB) plus live capture; signature 140×60 px (10–20 KB); thumb 240×240 px (20–50 KB); handwritten English declaration 800×400 px (50–100 KB). Other bank notices may differ.

2026 CBI recruitment photo and document guide ↗

Voter Form 6

The paper Form 6 PDF lists an unsigned colour photo box measuring 4.5×3.5 cm. The paper form does not specify a digital file-size limit; check the ECI portal for digital upload rules and orientation.

Passport Seva

The official passport guide says the uploaded photo should be unaltered by computer software. Many applicants are photographed at the Passport Seva Kendra; minors below four may need to bring a photo. Do not retouch a passport photo here.

GOOD TO KNOW

Keep the result valid and recognizable

Can Variuno create the live photo for an exam?

No. If the official portal requests live camera capture, capture it directly there. Variuno can prepare a separate scanned photo only when the form explicitly asks you to upload one.

What if my form's requirements are not listed?

Select Custom size in the image preparer and copy the width, height, minimum/maximum KB and file type from the active portal. If a dimension is given in centimetres, confirm the required DPI before converting it to pixels.

Can I edit or generate a signature or declaration?

The image tool only cleans, frames and compresses an image you provide. Use your own signature, fingerprint and handwriting. Never submit another person's writing or a generated identity photo.

Is a passport photo safe to edit?

The Passport Seva photo guide says the image should be unaltered by computer software, so do not use image retouching or background replacement for a passport submission.

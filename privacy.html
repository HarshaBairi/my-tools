Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Variuno Tools is a set of free tools that run in your web browser. This page explains what happens to your files and information.

Your files stay on your device

When you use a tool to resize, compress, convert or edit a file, the work happens inside your browser on your own phone or computer. Your files are not uploaded to our servers, and we do not keep copies of them. We have no accounts, so we do not collect names, passwords or sign-in details.

Things your browser may download

A few features need extra software or models that your browser downloads the first time you use them. Only the software is downloaded; your file is not sent anywhere.

  • Photo background removal: downloads an open-source model and code from Google-hosted servers (MediaPipe) and a public code delivery network (jsDelivr). These services may receive normal technical information such as your IP address, as any website you visit would.
  • Video and audio converter: downloads the converter program (ffmpeg, about 30 MB) from a public code delivery network (jsDelivr) the first time you convert. Your video or audio file is processed on your device and is not uploaded.
  • Text recognition (OCR): downloads the OCR engine and language data (Tesseract) from a public code delivery network.

Information stored in your browser

The application checklist and application-workspace progress, saved tools and recently opened tools are stored in your browser on your device. They contain checklist choices and tool IDs only; uploaded files are not stored there. You can remove this data by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Advertising

This site is free and may show ads to cover its costs. If ads are shown, advertising partners such as Google may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can manage or turn off personalised ads at Google Ads Settings. Ads are never shown inside a tool's result, and advertisers never receive your files.

Hosting and analytics

The site is delivered through a web hosting provider, which may keep standard server logs such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested. We do not use these logs to identify individual visitors.

Children

This site is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email or see the Contact page.
